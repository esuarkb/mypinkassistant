👜 Seminar Week

Your desk stays home. Your business doesn't. A customer texts you in the middle of a session asking for "that lipstick I got last time" — you don't need your laptop, your binder, or a quiet room to answer her.

Just type:
what did Jane Smith order

You'll get her order history right in the chat — what she bought and when. Then place the reorder in the same conversation, and it goes into InTouch for you. From a ballroom chair, in about a minute.

💗 Ways to Share

This is the week you'll be surrounded by consultants — and the week someone glances over and asks "wait, what is that?" Here's the easy answer, and you both get a free month out of it.

Just type:
referral

In person: your personal QR code appears right in the chat. Hold up your phone, she scans it, done — no spelling out a web address over a loud ballroom.

By text: the same bubble gives you your referral link. Copy it and send it to the two or three people you always end up explaining it to anyway.

In your unit: if your unit has a group chat or a Facebook group, that's the warmest place for it — from you, it's a recommendation, not an ad. (Check your group's rules first, of course.)

💡 Tip of the Week

Find the customers who quietly drifted off. Everybody has them — the ones who used to order like clockwork and then just… stopped. You don't have to scroll your whole list hunting for them.

Just type:
who hasn't ordered in 3 months

Change the number to whatever fits your business — 2 months, 6 months, a year. It's a good list to have waiting when you get home from Seminar with a fresh set of goals. 💕

✨ What's New

Discounts on your orders. Running a hostess special or giving a loyal customer a break? Say it the way you'd say it out loud, right in the order — no math on your end.

Try it:
new order for Jane Smith, 1 charcoal mask, 20% off
new order for Jane Smith, 1 charcoal mask, $10 off

A percentage or a flat dollar amount — whichever way you think about it. Either one works anywhere in the message.
